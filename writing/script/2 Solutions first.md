# Solutions first

[CARD: Solutions first]

[ANIM: a problem in, options out]

I use AI as a sparring partner. For my projects, for decisions, for everything I'm stuck on.

[Florian: one concrete sentence on what you use it for]

And for a long time, every conversation went the same way. I describe a problem. And I get options. Five ideas. Ten ideas. Each with pros and cons.

[ANIM: picking feels like progress]

And it feels great. I read them, I pick one, I ask for more. It feels like progress.

But after an hour I often had a long list, and no idea whether any of it was what I actually wanted.

[ANIM: don't jump into suggestions]

So my first reaction was simple. I told it: don't jump into suggestions. First help me understand the problem. Ask me questions instead.

That alone helped a lot. The conversations got slower. And better.
