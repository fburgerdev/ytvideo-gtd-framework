# Goal and steps

[CARD: Goal and steps]

[ANIM: a goal and steps]

So why does one page change so much? Here's what I think.

All strategy is really one thing: connecting a goal you have with steps you can take.

And humans are very bad at one half of that. Not at finding steps. At keeping the goal in mind while we take them.

[ANIM: the goal fades]

We start with a reason. Then we get busy. Each step makes sense after the one before. And slowly the reason we started fades, and we don't notice, because we're still moving.

[ANIM: the stages hold the thread]

The five stages are nothing more than a way to hold that thread. Every stage is tied to the one above it. The action belongs to a plan, the plan to a picture, the picture to a reason.

[ANIM: the AI jumps too]

And here's what I find most interesting. The AI makes the same mistake. It also jumps to solutions.

I suspect it learned that from us. We experience a list of options as helpful. So that's what it gives us. Reactive planning, at scale.
